Project

Video Resource Database for Help A Girl Out

How I built a 40-video resource database called The Equity Library for Help A Girl Out Canada from start to finish.

  1. 01 Strategy

    I understood the goals and audience

    With the Founder, I set the campaign objectives, understood stakeholder and funder goals, the target audience, timeline, and the platforms and formats that would carry it out.

  2. 02 Concept & Development

    I planned the scripts and shoots

    We did research and analysis on other similar videos first to ensure our USPs were highlighted. I created a content list and scripted over 50 videos for social media and long-format. I made sure to ask the right questions that audiences were looking for and built a project calendar for progress checking.

  3. 03 Filming Talent

    Organize and film

    Next, we found and coordinated with talent and influencers to this audience (doctors and community leaders), to film them interview-style at the Help A Girl Out Canada office.

  4. 04 Edit & Design

    Post-Production

    We went into editing mode where I edited, coloured and added music to multiple videos a week, maintaining content deadlines for posting on the database. I met weekly with our Founder for review sessions and feedback.

  5. 05 Publish

    Released across channels

    Finally, I used the content calendar to schedule the long-format videos to be released on YouTube, and short clips on social media as well as making announcements about the drops on LinkedIn and Facebook groups that matter to our audience. I analyzed the performance through YouTube analytics, and tracked metrics related to our overall marketing strategies and SEO via Google Analytics and shared these reports with the Founder.

  6. 06 Results

    Measured & learned

    I tracked performance against the original goal and shared the feedback with our team. We surpassed our targets, achieving a CTR of 3.7% and 33K views in our first 2 weeks.

See the final product and watch the videos I created for Help A Girl Out at theequitylibrary.ca.
